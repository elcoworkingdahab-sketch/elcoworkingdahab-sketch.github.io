Questions

Good to know

Can't find your answer? Message us on WhatsApp.

Do I need to book a desk?

No. Walk in during opening hours, pay for a pass, and take any free desk.

What are the opening hours?

Monday to Friday from 8 AM to midnight, and Saturday and Sunday from 9 AM to midnight. Passes cover the hours we're open.

How do I pay?

Cash or InstaPay at the desk. Prices are in Egyptian pounds or US dollars.

Where can I take calls?

Use the private meeting room. It fits one person and is free for members. Book a slot online.

Is the internet reliable?

Our main line gives up to 70 Mbps, and a second landline takes over if it drops.

Can I rent a monitor?

Yes. We have two monitors at 100 EGP a day each. Ask at the desk.
